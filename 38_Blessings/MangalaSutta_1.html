five types of actions committed by evil people

  1. The wicked person likes to entice and incite others to do evil, that is, the wicked person constantly tempts people to do bad, wrong, and erroneous things.
  2. Evil people often do things that are not their duty or responsibility.
  3. The wicked often have a mistaken perception, believing wrong to be right.
  4. Evil people, even if we speak truthfully or honestly, will still get angry.
  5. The wicked refuse to act in accordance with the rules and laws.
