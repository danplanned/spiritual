Types of Giving Leading to Different Realms in the Sensual Realm

Giving That Leads to the Four Woeful Realms (Hell, Hungry Ghosts, Animals, and Asuras):

  • Giving intoxicating substances
  • Organizing drinking parties
  • Giving a woman to a man for the purpose of satisfying sexual desire
  • Giving a bull to a cow / a hen to a rooster / a sow to a boar / etc.
  • Giving pornographic images or drawings that stimulate sexual desire
  • Giving chains or restraints for people to bind or imprison others
  • Giving false calculating devices, false weights, or false measuring tools for the purpose of cheating others
  • Giving poison
  • Giving money to those who are not permitted to accept money
  • Giving weapons

Human Realm:

  • Giving with the wish to be reborn as a human being (directing the merit toward being reborn as a human)
  • Keeping the Five Precepts purely
  • Serving and caring for one's parents without becoming discouraged or giving up
  • Knowing how to observe morality, practice spiritual development, give generously, etc.

Heaven of the Four Great Kings (Cātummahārājika):

  • Making merit with the wish to become a companion of the devas
  • Experiencing much suffering in this life and therefore giving with the wish to be reborn as a deva in the next life

Tāvatiṃsa Heaven:

  • Giving without wishing for rewards, but simply wishing to become a deva; the mind is not attached to the results
  • Giving simply because one enjoys giving

Yāma Heaven: (Giving Out of Habit)

  • Giving because of traditions passed down from one's grandparents or ancestors. For example, one's family practices giving, so one follows the tradition without really understanding what is needed, why one is giving, or what one is wishing for.

Tusita Heaven:

  • Giving in order to support and provide for the Saṅgha
  • Giving without seeking rewards and not merely out of habit

Nimmānaratī Heaven:

  • Giving because one understands that giving makes the mind peaceful, joyful, delighted, and allows wisdom to arise
  • Giving generously, accompanied by wisdom and an understanding of cause and effect

Paranimmitavasavattī Heaven:

  • Giving accompanied by wisdom, gladness, and joy
  • Giving with a pure mind
  • Giving like a spiritual practitioner or sage: giving purely and without being reluctant to give anything
